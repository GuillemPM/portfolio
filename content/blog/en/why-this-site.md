I have spent years shipping ERP extensions, integrations, and increasingly AI-assisted workflows for real companies. Most of that work stays private, which is how it should be.

This site is the public counterpart: a place to write down patterns that worked, mistakes I would not repeat, and the small implementation details that documentation often skips.

Expect posts about **Business Central**, **integrations**, and **software**. From a builder's perspective, not a sales pitch.

If something here saves you an afternoon of debugging, it did its job.
